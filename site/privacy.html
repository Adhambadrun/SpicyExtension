Privacy policy

For the SpicyExtension Chrome extension, version 1.0.0 · Last updated 12 September 2026

The short version. SpicyExtension does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. It has no server, no analytics and no network code of any kind. Everything it produces is created on your own machine, held in memory, and saved only when you choose to save it.

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to the SpicyExtension browser extension ("the extension") and to this website. The extension is open source; its complete source code is published at github.com/Adhambadrun/SpicyExtension, so every statement below can be independently verified rather than taken on trust.

2. What the extension collects

Nothing is collected by the developer. No personal information, no usage statistics, no crash reports, no identifiers and no analytics are gathered, received or stored by us. There is no account to create and no data to delete, because none is ever sent.

When you explicitly start a capture, the extension reads part of the page you are viewing and turns it into JSON on your own device. That JSON is shown to you and held in the panel's memory. It is not transmitted anywhere. It becomes a file only if you press the download button, or text on your clipboard if you press copy.

What a capture contains

  • The visible text of the single page area you selected.
  • A short allowlist of structural attributes: class names, roles, accessibility labels, datetime values and test IDs.
  • The origin and route of the page (for example /flights) — never the query string or fragment.
  • A timestamp and counts of how many elements were captured, omitted or redacted.

What is excluded from a capture

  • Scripts, styles, frames and embedded media.
  • Hidden, inert and aria-hidden subtrees.
  • Form controls and their values — field values are never read.
  • Link destinations, resource URLs, element IDs, event handlers and arbitrary data attributes.
  • Email addresses, URLs, bearer tokens and common credential patterns found in visible text are automatically replaced.

3. What the extension never accesses

  • Cookies and authentication. The extension has no cookies permission and never reads, copies or transmits your login. Signing in happens entirely between you, Chrome and the website.
  • Local and session storage. Never read.
  • Passwords and form input. Never read.
  • Other websites. The extension declares a single host permission, https://agentsearch.vercel.app/*, and has no access to any other site, to your browsing history, to your tabs in general, to your bookmarks, or to other browser profiles.
  • Your page, unprompted. The content script stays dormant until you press the capture button. It does not read the page on load and never captures in the background.

4. Data storage and retention

The extension stores nothing. It requests no storage permission and writes to no database, no cloud service and no synced profile data. A capture exists only in the panel's memory and is discarded as soon as you clear it, close the panel, reload the page or navigate away.

Files you download and text you copy are ordinary files and clipboard contents under your own control. The extension cannot read, retrieve or erase them afterwards, so please store and share them carefully.

5. Data transmission and third parties

The extension contains no network client. There is no fetch, no XMLHttpRequest, no WebSocket, no EventSource and no sendBeacon in the shipped code — the build fails if any of them appears. Its packaged pages are additionally locked down with the content security policy connect-src 'none', which forbids outbound connections at the browser level.

As a result: no data is sold, rented, traded, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, credit assessment, profiling or training. There are no third-party services, SDKs, trackers, remote fonts or remotely loaded code in the extension. All code is bundled at build time.

The website you are reading serves only static files and sets no cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, which we do not use to identify anyone.

6. Limits you should know about

Automatic redaction is best effort, not complete anonymization. Recognizable email addresses, URLs and common credential patterns are replaced, but personal information in ordinary visible text — names, booking references, phone numbers or unusual identifiers — can remain in a capture. Review the JSON before you export it, and again before you share it with anyone. The export buttons are deliberately disabled until you confirm you have done so.

Content inside shadow roots is not included, and offscreen content is not necessarily excluded; the extension is not a visual screenshot tool or a complete visibility audit.

7. Children

The extension is a developer utility, is not directed at children, and knowingly collects no information from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or delete on request. You remain in full control: uninstalling the extension removes it entirely, and any files you exported are deleted the same way you delete any other file on your computer.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new date, and the change will be visible in the public repository's history. Material changes to how the extension handles data would also require a new extension version and a fresh Chrome Web Store review.

10. Contact

Questions, concerns or security reports are welcome through the public issue tracker at github.com/Adhambadrun/SpicyExtension/issues, or via the contact address listed on the support page.
